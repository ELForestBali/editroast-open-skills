These RU/EN/ES forms reproduce the reviewed historical EditRoast mappings, with `Chief Sterling` added as the approved English chair display for the existing `editorial_board` role. For another locale, retain the English display names or use a human-reviewed locale pack. Do not invent a new alias and present it as established localization. Project state records the stable ID, never a translated string.
