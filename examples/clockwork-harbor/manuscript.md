# Chapter 1 — The Bell Below

At six every morning, Mara Venn wound the harbor clock. At six every morning, the sea answered with a bell no one else could hear.

On the day the north pier vanished, the bell rang seven times.

Mara counted twice, though counting could not restore a pier. Fog covered the water where the warehouses should have stood. Dockmaster Ilyan waited below the clock tower, hat crushed between both hands.

“You were up there,” he said. “Tell me what you saw.”

“Nothing.” The lie tasted of brass.

In her pocket, a gear from the clock turned by itself.

# Chapter 2 — A Map That Moves

The city archive kept maps in drawers labeled by year, fire, and flood. Archivist Soren found the missing pier on a chart dated thirty years in the future.

Mara laid the warm gear over the paper. Ink streets shifted beneath it. A narrow line ran from the clock tower to an island beyond the breakwater, although every sailor in Bellweather knew there was no island there.

“Your father drew this symbol,” Soren said, pointing to a black spiral.

Mara had been told her father drowned before she could remember him. She had also been told the harbor clock never lost a minute.

Outside, the clock struck noon eleven times.

# Chapter 3 — The Eighth Bell

At dusk, Mara and Soren rowed beyond the breakwater. The impossible island appeared when the tower disappeared behind fog.

They found the north pier intact on the island, along with every person who had stood on it that morning. None believed more than ten minutes had passed.

Mara fitted the loose gear into a rusted mechanism at the pier's end. The sea bell began its seventh peal.

“Pull it back out,” Soren said.

Mara thought of her father, the moving map, and the clock that had lied for thirty years. She turned the gear forward.

The eighth bell did not sound through the water. It sounded beneath the city.
